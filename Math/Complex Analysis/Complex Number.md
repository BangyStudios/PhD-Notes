## Definition
From [Halburd2009ComplexAnalysis](Halburd2009ComplexAnalysis.pdf):
A complex number is an ordered set of real numbers $(x, y)$. The space of all complex numbers is denoted $\mathbb{C}$ and has the following operations defined on it:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x_1, y_1) + (x_2, y_2) &:= (x_1 + x_2, y_1 + y_2) \\
(x_1, y_1)(x_2, y_2) &:= (x_1x_2 - y_1y_2, x_1y_2 + x_2y_1) \\
(x, y)^{-1} &:= \left( \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2}, -\frac{y}{x^2 + y^2}\right), \quad (x, y) \neq (0, 0)
\end{aligned}
$$
Every complex number $(x, y)$ can then be written as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
z &= x + iy \\
&= r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta) \\
&= re^{i\theta}
\end{aligned}
$$ where, according to Euler's formula:
$$
e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta
$$
