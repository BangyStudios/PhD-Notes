## Definition
A [complex number](Complex%20Number.md) is a value $c = a + bi$ where $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ are the real and imaginary parts and $i = \sqrt{-1}$.

A complex vector space $\mathbb{C}^d$ is the $d$-dimensional space of complex-valued vectors — each of the $d$ components is a complex number:
$$
\mathbf{c} = \begin{bmatrix} a_1 + b_1 i \\ \vdots \\ a_d + b_d i \end{bmatrix}, \quad a_k, b_k \in \mathbb{R}
$$

This extends $\mathbb{R}^d$: a real vector is a special case where every $b_k = 0$.

### Example in $\mathbb{C}^2$
$$
\mathbf{c} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 + 2i \\ -1 + 4i \end{bmatrix}
$$
Real parts: $\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$, imaginary parts: $\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 4\end{bmatrix}$.
